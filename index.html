FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to us.

How long does a website take to build?

A marketing website typically takes 3–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger eCommerce sites and custom web applications are scoped in phases, usually 8–16 weeks.

Which platform should I choose — WordPress, Laravel or Next.js?

WordPress is great when your team wants to edit content easily. Laravel suits custom business logic and portals. Next.js / React is ideal for high-performance, app-like experiences. We'll recommend the right fit after understanding your goals.

When will I see results from SEO?

Technical fixes can show impact within weeks, but meaningful organic growth usually builds over 3–6 months. We report progress monthly so you always know where you stand.

Do you manage ad budgets and campaigns end to end?

Yes — strategy, creatives, landing pages, tracking setup, launch and ongoing optimisation across Google, Meta, LinkedIn and YouTube.

Do you offer support after launch?

Absolutely. We offer maintenance and hosting plans covering updates, backups, security monitoring and small content changes.
